# TailorApply — 6-Week SEO Content Calendar

Niche wedge for launch: **career switchers moving into tech/data roles** (narrow enough to rank,
big enough to matter — broaden after month 2).

Format: every post follows the same template so you (or an AI draft + your edit pass) can produce
2–3/week in under an hour each:
`[Problem] → [Why generic advice fails] → [Concrete example, before/after] → [Soft CTA to tool]`

## Week 1 — Foundational, high-intent queries
1. "How to tailor your resume for every job application (without rewriting it from scratch)"
2. "Cover letter for a career switcher with no direct experience"
3. "ATS resume keywords: what they actually are and how to find them in a job posting"

## Week 2 — Role-specific long tail (repeat this pattern per role, this is the scalable part)
4. "Cover letter for a Data Analyst role — no experience"
5. "How to tailor a resume for a Junior Product Manager job"
6. "Resume bullet examples: before and after tailoring to a job description"

## Week 3 — Comparison / consideration-stage
7. "Resume tailoring by hand vs. AI: what actually changes in an ATS score"
8. "Best free tools for job seekers in 2026"
9. "Do I need a different resume for every job? (short answer: yes — here's the fast way)"

## Week 4 — Programmatic SEO seed set
Build ONE page template: `/cover-letter-for/[job-title]` — auto-populate with role-specific
tailored examples generated once (not live API calls) and lightly reviewed. Seed with the 20
highest-search-volume entry-level titles (Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Software Engineer New
Grad, Customer Success, Marketing Coordinator, etc.). This is the highest-leverage SEO lever
available and needs almost no ongoing writing once templated.

## Week 5 — Community-adjacent / linkable
10. "We analyzed 200 rejected resumes — here's what they had in common"
11. "The exact prompt structure that tailors a resume without inventing experience" (transparency
    post — builds trust, links to the tool naturally)

## Week 6 — Bottom-of-funnel
12. "TailorApply vs. [Competitor]" (only publish once you have a real, fair comparison)
13. "How long should a tailored resume take? (and why 30 minutes per application doesn't scale)"

## Distribution rule of thumb
- Every post gets cross-posted as a short thread/video hook for TikTok/Shorts and one relevant
  subreddit comment (only where genuinely useful, never as a drive-by link drop).
- Track which titles bring signups in Vercel/Plausible analytics; double down on the pattern that
  works instead of spreading evenly across topics.
